OpenPrintHQ Support

Help with the OpenPrintHQ apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Android, the installer and the hub.

Contact

Email norjms@gmail.com. Say which app or part you are using, its version, and what you expected to happen. We answer within a few working days.

Report a problem or ask for a feature

Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/norjms/openprinthq/issues. Leave out passwords, keys and your hub's address.

Getting started with the app

  1. The app needs an OpenPrintHQ hub, which you run on your own hardware. The hub is open source: github.com/norjms/openprinthq.
  2. On your hub, open Connect a phone.
  3. In the app, scan the QR code it shows. You can add more than one hub.

Common questions

The app cannot reach my hub. Check that the phone can open your hub's address in its web browser. On iPhone, allow Local Network for OpenPrintHQ in Settings if your hub is on your home or shop network.

I do not get notifications. Turn them on in the app, and check that your hub has phone notifications switched on under Farm, Settings, Phone app notifications.

Refunds. Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play; ask Apple or Google for a refund.
