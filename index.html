Run a 3D print farm as a product business.

OpenPrintHQ manages every model as a product: photos, print settings, files, colour options, bill of materials, store listing and supply levels. The print farm, the model library, filament, parts and your storefront are all driven from one place, on your own hardware.

What it does

One hub in front of everything a print business runs on.

Models as products

Each model carries its photos, print settings, files, colour options and bill of materials, ready to list in your store.

The print farm

Queue, printers, cameras and maintenance for Bambu Lab, Prusa, Klipper and OctoPrint machines, with plate checks after every print.

Filament and parts

Spools and stock tracked as you print, so you know what is low before an order needs it.

Your storefront

Connects to WooCommerce: availability, instant quotes and orders flow between the store and the farm.

Slice from OrcaSlicer

OrcaSlicer in the browser comes connected, and the Print Queue add-on sends jobs from OrcaSlicer on your own computer.

Printers anywhere

OpenPrintHQ Link reaches printers on other networks by connecting out, so nothing at home is exposed.
