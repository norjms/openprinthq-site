OpenPrintHQ Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Who we are

OpenPrintHQ is made by James Norman, Altoona, Iowa, United States ("we", "us"). This policy covers the OpenPrintHQ apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Android, the OpenPrintHQ installer, the push notification relay we run, and the website openprinthq.com.

Contact: norjms@gmail.com

The short version

  • OpenPrintHQ is self-hosted. Your printers, jobs, files, filament, orders and accounts live on the hub you run, not on our servers.
  • The apps talk to your hub. They do not send your data to us.
  • We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share personal data.
  • The few things that do reach us are listed below: sealed notifications passing through our push relay, and an installer license acceptance record that contains no name, email or address.

Your hub

The OpenPrintHQ hub is software you install and run on your own machine. Whatever the hub stores (sign-ins, people on your team, printers, print jobs, model files, camera views, filament, orders and customer details from your shop) is stored by you, on your machine, and we have no access to it. If you run a hub for other people, you are responsible for their data and for telling them how you handle it.

The apps

The apps connect only to the hub address you give them (by typing it or scanning your hub's QR code).

  • Sign-in: the key your hub issues to the app is kept in the phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android's encrypted storage) and sent only to your hub.
  • Camera: used to scan your hub's QR codes, spool and shelf labels and barcodes, and to photograph filament packaging. Photos are not uploaded to us.
  • Photo library: read only when you pick a photo of filament packaging.
  • Local network: used to reach a hub on your home or shop network.
  • Filament identification: a barcode is looked up first in your hub's own library, then by your hub in an online product database (UPCitemdb), which receives only the barcode number. A packaging photo is read on the phone (Apple's on-device models and text recognition, or Google ML Kit on Android). If that does not identify it, the photo goes to your hub's own vision model if your hub has one set up. It never goes to us.
  • Purchases: you buy the app through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we receive sales reports without your card details.

Notifications

Notifications are off until you turn them on. When you do:

  1. Your phone makes a key pair. The private key never leaves the phone.
  2. Your phone gives your hub its public key and the push token Apple or Google assigned to it.
  3. For each alert, your hub seals the title, text and link so only your phone can open them, and hands the sealed message to our push relay, which passes it to Apple's or Google's push service.

The relay sees the push token and sealed bytes it cannot read, and keeps nothing. Your hub can be set to use a relay you run yourself instead. Turning notifications off, or removing the phone, makes your hub forget its key and token.

The relay has no database, files or request log. It can keep a delivery log for testing, which is off unless turned on; when on, it records one line per delivery (the time, the platform, a short one-way hash of the push token, the size and the result), never the message and never an IP address.

The Android app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to receive notifications. Google's handling of push tokens is covered by Google's privacy policy.

The installer

Before installing anything, the installer asks you to accept its license agreement and sends us a record of that acceptance, which we keep so we can show who accepted which terms. The record contains only: the agreement's version and fingerprint, the time, the installer's version, and a fingerprint of the installation (a one-way hash of a random identifier and the identifier Docker gives the machine). It contains no name, email, address or anything you typed. It is received by our service on Cloudflare and stored in a private GitHub repository.

The installer and the hub send no telemetry or usage data. If optional telemetry is ever added, it will be off unless you turn it on, and this policy will describe it first.

The website

openprinthq.com is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser and pages requested) for security and operation, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Children

OpenPrintHQ is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

Because your data is on the hub you run, you can see, export, correct and delete it there. For anything we hold (an acceptance record, or a question you emailed us), contact us at the address above. Wherever you live, including under the GDPR and California law, you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. We will answer within 30 days.

Changes

We will give at least 30 days' notice before any change to this policy takes effect, by posting the new policy on this page with its new date and a note of what changed, and, where we can, in the apps and the installer.

A new version of this policy applies to you only once you accept it or reinstall OpenPrintHQ. Until then, the version you last accepted continues to apply to you.
